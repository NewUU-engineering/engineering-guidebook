# Signatories

The people below have publicly stated that they follow the [Engineering Guidebook](https://newuu-engineering.github.io/engineering-guidebook/).

Adoption is voluntary. Signing means you have read the guidebook, you think it is right, and you intend to act on it — not that you agree with every sentence. If you disagree with a sentence, [propose a change](docs/contributing.md).

## How to sign

Open a pull request adding one line to the list below, in this format:

```
- Name — role, department or programme, year (optional link)
```

For example:

```
- Aziza Karimova — BSc Mechanical Engineering, 2027
- Jane Doe — Department of Physics
```

You may remove your name at any time, in the same way, without giving a reason.
